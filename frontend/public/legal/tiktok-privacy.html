ScoopFeeds Publisher — Privacy Policy

Covers the ScoopFeeds Publisher application and its use of the TikTok Content Posting API.

Last updated: 20 August 2026

In short: ScoopFeeds Publisher collects no personal data from anyone. It connects to one TikTok account — our own — and uses that connection only to upload our own videos.

1. What the application is

ScoopFeeds Publisher is an internal tool operated by ScoopFeeds, the publisher of scoopfeeds.com. It uploads ScoopFeeds' own original news-explainer videos to ScoopFeeds' own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public: there is no sign-up and no user accounts.

2. What it connects to

The application authorises against a single TikTok account — the ScoopFeeds account — using TikTok's OAuth (Login Kit) with the video.publish scope. That scope permits publishing videos to the authorising account. It does not grant access to other users' accounts, videos, comments, followers or messages, and we request no scope that would.

3. What we store

  • OAuth tokens — an access token and a refresh token for the ScoopFeeds TikTok account. These are held in a configuration file on our own build machine and are used solely to authenticate publishing requests for our own videos.
  • Publish identifiers — the publish_id TikTok returns for each upload, kept alongside the video project so we can check whether a post completed.

That is the complete list. No other data is retained by the application.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store or process personal data about TikTok users.
  • We do not read, scrape or analyse other people's accounts, videos or comments.
  • We do not track anyone, and we run no analytics inside the application.
  • These two pages set no cookies and require no JavaScript to read. They are static HTML and load no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, no third-party fonts and no embedded media. The site is served through a CDN (Cloudflare), which may add its own email-address obfuscation script to pages containing a contact address; it sets no cookies and performs no tracking.
  • We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties, and we do not use any data for advertising or for training machine-learning models.

5. Access, revocation and deletion

Because the only account involved is our own, the account holder controls access directly:

  • Revoking the application in TikTok's own settings (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions) immediately invalidates our tokens.
  • Emailing us at the address below will have any stored tokens deleted from our machine.

Tokens are also discarded whenever they are rotated or the integration is retired. Deleting them does not remove videos already published to TikTok; those are managed in the TikTok account itself.

6. Security

Credentials are kept outside the application's source repository and are not committed to version control. They live on a single machine under the account holder's control.
