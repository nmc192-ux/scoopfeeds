ScoopFeeds Publisher — Terms of Service

Applies to the ScoopFeeds Publisher application and its use of the TikTok Content Posting API.

Last updated: 20 August 2026

1. Who operates this service

ScoopFeeds Publisher is an internal tool built and operated by ScoopFeeds, the publisher of scoopfeeds.com. Its only function is to upload ScoopFeeds' own original news-explainer videos to ScoopFeeds' own TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API. It is not a product offered to the public: there is no sign-up, no user accounts, and no way for anyone outside ScoopFeeds to use it.

2. What it publishes

The application publishes only content that ScoopFeeds owns or is licensed to use. Our videos are produced in-house: the scripts are written by us, the graphics are rendered by us, and any stock footage is licensed for the purpose. Every video is reviewed and approved by a person at ScoopFeeds before it is published. Nothing is posted automatically without that approval.

3. Compliance with TikTok's rules

ScoopFeeds Publisher is operated in compliance with TikTok's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines and Developer Terms. We use the API only for the purpose it was granted for — publishing our own videos to our own account — and we observe TikTok's posting limits. We do not use the API to access, collect or analyse other people's content or accounts.

4. Accuracy and corrections

Our videos are journalism, and we source them. Where a video states a figure, it states the institution and date it came from. If we get something wrong, we correct it and say so. To raise a correction, email us at the address below.

5. Availability and changes

This is an internal tool with no service commitment to third parties. We may change, suspend or retire it at any time. If these terms change, the "last updated" date above changes with them.
